## Derived fact 1 — r * 0 = 0

**Coordinate.** Ring · multiplication · zero on the right gives zero · **Derived fact**

*Source: dummit-foote*

*Built on: zero on the left gives zero, for multiplication on Ring, order does not matter, for addition on Ring*

> **Goal.** r * 0 = 0
>
> $$\forall r \in Ring\quad r \cdot 0 = 0$$

| ④ | From step 2 and step 3, this implies r times 0 equals 0. Hence proven. | ④ | $r \cdot 0 = 0$ |
