**Coordinate.** Rat · multiplication · one on the left gives the value · **Derived fact**

*Source: landau*

*Built on: one is the right identity, for multiplication on Rat, order does not matter, for multiplication on Rat*

> **Goal.** 1 * q = q

> $$\forall q \in Rat\quad 1 \cdot q = q$$
