**Coordinate.** Rat · addition · zero on the left gives the value · **Derived fact**

*Source: landau*

*Built on: zero on the right gives the value, for addition on Rat, order does not matter, for addition on Rat*

> **Goal.** 0 + q = q

> $$\forall q \in Rat\quad 0 + q = q$$
