**Coordinate.** GroupHom · identity · the identity image is a right unit · **Derived fact**

*Source: dummit-foote*

*Built on: the identity image multiplies to itself, for identity on GroupHom*

> **Goal.** f(1) * f(1) = f(1)
>
> $$\forall f \in GroupHom\quad f(1) * f(1) = f(1)$$
