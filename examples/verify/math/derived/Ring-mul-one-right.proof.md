**Coordinate.** Ring · multiplication · one is the right identity · **Derived fact**

*Source: dummit-foote*

*Built on: one is the left identity, for multiplication on Ring, parentheses do not matter, for multiplication on Ring*

> **Goal.** r * 1 = r
>
> $$\forall r \in Ring\quad r \cdot 1 = r$$
