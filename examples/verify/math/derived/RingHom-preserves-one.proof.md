**Coordinate.** RingHom · multiplication · one maps to one · **Derived fact**

*Source: dummit-foote*

*Built on: products map to products, for multiplication on RingHom, one is the left identity, for multiplication on Ring*

> **Goal.** f(1) = 1
>
> $$\forall f \in RingHom\quad f(1) = 1$$
