## Derived fact 1 — 1 * m = m

**Coordinate.** the integers · multiplication · one is the left identity · **Derived fact**

*Source: landau*

*Built on: one is the right identity, for multiplication on the integers, order does not matter, for multiplication on the integers*

> **Goal.** 1 * m = m
>
> $$\forall m \in \mathbb{Z}\quad 1 \cdot m = m$$

| | **Proof** | | **Math** |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| ① | We prove that one is the left identity for multiplication on the integers. |  |  |
| ② | We invoke the derived fact governing multiplication on the integers: one is the right identity, for multiplication on the integers (instantiated for m). |  |  |
| ③ | We invoke the derived fact governing multiplication on the integers: order does not matter, for multiplication on the integers (instantiated for 1, m). |  |  |
| ④ | From step 2 and step 3, this implies 1 times m equals m. Hence proven. | ④ | $1 \cdot m = m$ |
