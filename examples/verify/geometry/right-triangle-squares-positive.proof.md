## Derived fact 1 — a squared is greater than zero

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · right triangle · leg squares are positive · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: leg square is less than hypotenuse square, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane, the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs*

> **Goal.** a squared is greater than zero
>
> $$a^{2} > 0$$

| | **Proof** | | **Math** |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| ① | We prove that leg squares are positive for right triangles in the Euclidean plane. |  |  |
| ② | We invoke the derived fact governing right triangles in the Euclidean plane: leg square is less than hypotenuse square, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane. |  |  |
| ③ | We invoke the derived fact governing right triangles in the Euclidean plane: the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs. |  |  |
| ④ | From step 2 and step 3, this implies a times a > 0. Hence proven. | ④ | $a^{2} > 0$ |
