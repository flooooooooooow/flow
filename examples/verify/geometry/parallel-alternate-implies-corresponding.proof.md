*Equal alternate angles imply equal corresponding angles.*

**Source.** euclid — Elements, Book I, Proposition 29

## Derived fact 1 — corresponding angles equal when alternate angles equal

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · parallel lines · alternate equal implies corresponding equal · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: alternate interior angles are equal when parallel lines meet a transversal, corresponding angles are equal, for parallel lines in on the Euclidean plane*

> **Goal.** corresponding angles equal when alternate angles equal
>
> $$\angle \text{corresponding alpha} = \angle \text{corresponding beta}$$
