## Derived fact 1 — if side AB equals side AC then angle at B equals angle at C

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · isosceles triangle · equal sides imply equal base angles · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: the angles at the base of an isosceles triangle are equal, two sides and the included angle determine a triangle up to congruence*

> **Goal.** if side AB equals side AC then angle at B equals angle at C
>
> $$\angle B = \angle C$$
