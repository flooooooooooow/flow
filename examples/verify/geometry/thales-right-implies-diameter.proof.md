# examples.verify.geometry

*A right inscribed angle implies the subtending chord is a diameter.*

**Source.** euclid — Elements, Book III, Proposition 31

## Derived fact 1 — chord AB is a diameter when angle ACB is right

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · circle · right inscribed implies diameter chord · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: semicircle inscribed angle is right witness, for circles in on the Euclidean plane, central angle on diameter is two right, for circles in on the Euclidean plane*

> **Goal.** chord AB is a diameter when angle ACB is right
>
> $$\text{chord AB is diameter}$$
