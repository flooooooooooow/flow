## Derived fact 1 — 10 squared equals 6 squared plus 8 squared

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · right triangle · six eight ten satisfies Pythagoras · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: five twelve thirteen satisfies Pythagoras, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane, the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs*

> **Goal.** 10 squared equals 6 squared plus 8 squared
>
> $$10^{2} = 6^{2} + 8^{2}$$
