*A chord with the centre forms an isosceles triangle.*

**Source.** euclid — Elements, Book I, Definition 15

## Derived fact 1 — OA equals OB for chord AB through centre O

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · circle · chord through centre gives isosceles triangle · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: all radii from the centre to the circumference are equal*

> **Goal.** OA equals OB for chord AB through centre O
>
> $$OA = OB$$
