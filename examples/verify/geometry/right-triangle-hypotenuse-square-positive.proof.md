## Derived fact 1 — c squared is greater than zero

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · right triangle · hypotenuse square is positive · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: leg squares are positive, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane, the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs*

> **Goal.** c squared is greater than zero
