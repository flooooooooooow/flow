## Derived fact 1 — co-interior alpha plus beta equals two right angles

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · parallel lines · co-interior angles sum to two right · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: corresponding angles are equal, for parallel lines in on the Euclidean plane, adjacent angles on a line sum to two right, for intersecting lines in on the Euclidean plane*

> **Goal.** co-interior alpha plus beta equals two right angles
>
> $$\angle \text{co interior alpha} + \angle \text{co interior beta} = 180^\circ$$
