## Derived fact 1 — 29 squared equals 20 squared plus 21 squared

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · right triangle · twenty twenty one twenty nine satisfies Pythagoras · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: seven twenty four twenty five satisfies Pythagoras, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane, the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs*

> **Goal.** 29 squared equals 20 squared plus 21 squared
