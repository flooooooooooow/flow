## Derived fact 1 — The interior angles of a triangle sum to two right angles

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · triangle · interior angles sum to two right angles · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid — Elements, Book I, Proposition 32*

*Built on: alternate interior angles are equal when parallel lines meet a transversal*

> **Goal.** The interior angles of a triangle sum to two right angles
>
> $$\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ$$

| | **Proof** | | **Math** |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| ① | We prove that interior angles sum to two right angles for triangles in the Euclidean plane. |  |  |
| ② | We invoke the axiom governing parallel lines in the Euclidean plane: alternate interior angles are equal when parallel lines meet a transversal. |  |  |
| ③ | From step 2, a line through the apex parallel to the base makes alternate angles with the sides, so the three interior angles line up on a straight line, so angle A plus angle B plus angle C equals two right angles. Hence proven. | ③ | $\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ$ |
