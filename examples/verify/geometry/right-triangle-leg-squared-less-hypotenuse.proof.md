## Derived fact 1 — a squared is less than c squared

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · right triangle · leg square is less than hypotenuse square · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs, each leg is shorter than hypotenuse, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane*

> **Goal.** a squared is less than c squared
>
> $$a^{2} < c^{2}$$
