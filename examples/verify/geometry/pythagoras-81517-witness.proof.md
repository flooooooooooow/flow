## Derived fact 1 — 17 squared equals 8 squared plus 15 squared

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · right triangle · eight fifteen seventeen satisfies Pythagoras · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: nine twelve fifteen satisfies Pythagoras, for right triangles in on the Euclidean plane, the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares on the legs*

> **Goal.** 17 squared equals 8 squared plus 15 squared
>
> $$17^{2} = 8^{2} + 15^{2}$$
