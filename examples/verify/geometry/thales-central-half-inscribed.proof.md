## Derived fact 1 — half of angle AOB equals angle ACB

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · circle · half central on diameter gives inscribed · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: thales inscribed is half central, for circles in on the Euclidean plane, central angle on diameter is two right, for circles in on the Euclidean plane*
