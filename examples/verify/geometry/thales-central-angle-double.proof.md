**Source.** euclid — Elements, Book III, Proposition 31

## Derived fact 1 — angle AOB equals two right angles for diameter AB

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · circle · central angle on diameter is two right · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*
