## Derived fact 1 — if angle at B equals angle at C then side AB equals side AC

**Coordinate.** the Euclidean plane · isosceles triangle · equal base angles imply equal sides · **Derived fact**

*Source: euclid*

*Built on: the angles at the base of an isosceles triangle are equal, two sides and the included angle determine a triangle up to congruence*

> **Goal.** if angle at B equals angle at C then side AB equals side AC
