## Findings

### CI Hardening Gaps (Medium)
1. **Dependencies are unpinned in CI**  
   - Evidence: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` installs `pytest` without version pinning; no lockfile present.  
   - Risk: nondeterministic CI and potential supply-chain drift.  
   - Recommendation: introduce a lock (e.g., `requirements.txt` with pinned versions, or a `pip-tools`/`uv` lock), and use it in CI.

2. **Linting is syntax-only**  
   - Evidence: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` uses `py_compile` only.  
   - Risk: style and type regressions not caught; shell scripts not checked.  
   - Recommendation: add `ruff` (or `flake8`), `mypy`, and `shellcheck` where applicable.

3. **Missing security scanning in CI**  
   - Evidence: no SAST or dependency scanning jobs in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.  
   - Risk: regressions or vulnerable dependencies slip through.  
   - Recommendation: add CodeQL and dependency review or `pip-audit` (or equivalent).
